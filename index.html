FAQ

1. Are these fees exact?

They're estimates based on each platform's published standard rates for US sellers on the date shown. Category exceptions, store subscriptions, optional ads, international sales and currency conversion aren't included. Always check the platform's own fee page before pricing.

2. Why does shipping change the fees?

Several marketplaces calculate their percentage fees on the total the buyer pays, including shipping. eBay's final value fee and Etsy's transaction fee both include shipping, so a higher shipping charge raises the fee even though that money goes toward postage.

3. Which marketplace has the lowest fees?

It depends on the sale price and how you get paid, so run your own numbers above. If you're looking for a marketplace with lowest fees, remember fees are only one factor: buyer traffic, your category and how easily you can move your listings matter too. TrueGether, for example, was built as an eBay alternative with no marketplace fees, but you still pay your payment provider's processing fee.

4. Is payment processing included?

Each platform handles it differently. Etsy charges a separate payment processing fee. TrueGether charges no marketplace fees, but the payment provider charges its own processing fee, estimated here at about 2.9% + $0.30. The provider's rate can change from time to time. The eBay estimate uses the final value fee and per-order fee from eBay's selling fees page.

5. How often are the rates updated?

The rates were last checked on October 13, 2026. Platforms change fees from time to time, so the methodology page links to every official source.
