How eBay fees work in 2026

Most eBay sales pay two fees. The final value fee is a percentage of the total sale: 13.6% in most categories, up to $7,500 per item, and 2.35% on any part above that. Then there's a per-order fee of $0.40, or $0.30 if the order total is $10 or less.

The total sale includes the item price, buyer-paid shipping and sales tax. So if you charge $8 for shipping, eBay takes 13.6% of that $8 too. You also get 250 free listings a month; after that, each new listing costs $0.35 in most categories.

Paying too much? On a typical $58 sale, eBay fees come to $8.29, while the same sale on TrueGether's free plan costs about $1.98 in payment processing. If you're weighing up eBay alternatives, test one with a few listings alongside your eBay shop rather than moving everything at once.

Ways to keep more of each eBay sale

  • Bundle small items. The per-order fee is charged once per order, so one $20 bundle beats four $5 sales.
  • Charge what postage really costs. eBay's fee applies to buyer-paid shipping, so padding shipping doesn't help you.
  • Protect your seller rating. Falling to Below Standard adds 6% to your final value fees.
  • Run a second channel for higher-priced items. A few percentage points become real money on a $300 sale. TrueGether, for example, can import your existing eBay listings, so trying it doesn't mean retyping your inventory.

FAQ

How much does eBay take from a sale in 2026?

In most categories, 13.6% of the total sale (up to $7,500) plus a $0.40 per-order fee, or $0.30 if the order is $10 or less. On a $100 sale, that's $14.00.

Does the eBay fee include shipping and sales tax?

Yes. eBay calculates the final value fee on the total the buyer pays, including buyer-paid shipping and sales tax.

Which eBay categories have lower fees?

Guitars and basses (6.7%), athletic shoes at $150 and over (8%, with no per-order fee), and trading cards, comics and most coins (13.25%).

How accurate is this eBay fee calculator?

It uses eBay's published US rates, checked in October 2026, for standard sellers. It doesn't include promoted listings, store subscription discounts, optional upgrades or international fees, so always confirm on eBay's own fee page before you price.
