Methodology and sources

All rates below are each platform's published standard rates for US sellers, last checked October 13, 2026. The calculator gives estimates for a single, standard sale. It doesn't include category exceptions, store subscriptions, optional advertising, international or currency fees.

Formulas

eBay

total = price + shipping + tax
final value fee = 13.6% × min(total, 7,500) + 2.35% × max(total − 7,500, 0)
per-order fee = 0.30 if total ≤ 10, otherwise 0.40

Etsy

listing fee = 0.20
transaction fee = 6.5% × (price + shipping)
processing = 3% × (price + shipping + tax) + 0.25

TrueGether

marketplace fees = 0
processing = PayPal rate × (price + shipping + tax) + PayPal fixed fee

What you keep

you keep = price + shipping − total fees. Sales tax is passed on to the state, so it's not counted as your income, but it's included where a platform charges fees on it.
